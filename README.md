### 🚀 Flagship Deep Dives

#### 1. [ArchLens AI](https://github.com/irtazirfan08-source/archlens-ai)
* **Domain:** Cloud Architecture Verification & Topology Generation
* **Capabilities:** Automated anti-pattern auditing (SPOFs, unthrottled queues, unindexed reads), cost/scalability grading, and instant bidirectional **Mermaid.js** diagram generation.

#### 2. [IncidentPulse AI](https://github.com/irtazirfan08-source/incidentpulse-ai)
* **Domain:** Autonomous SRE Incident Investigation & RCA
* **Capabilities:** Analyzes distributed telemetry spikes, applies statistical anomaly scoring via Wasserstein distance, isolates blast radiuses, and generates mitigation runbooks.

#### 3. [DocuAssist RAG](https://github.com/irtazirfan08-source/docuassist-ai)
* **Domain:** Enterprise Customer Support & Grounded Retrieval
* **Capabilities:** Hybrid vector search, strict hallucination guards with automatic Tier-2 escalation, sub-2ms retrieval latency, and exact source citation attribution.

#### 4. [Football Vision Engine](https://github.com/irtazirfan08-source/football_vision_engine)
* **Domain:** Computer Vision & Real-Time Object Tracking
* **Capabilities:** PyTorch YOLO and ByteTrack pipeline for tracking players, referees, and ball trajectories in broadcast video streams with speed and spatial heatmap overlays.
